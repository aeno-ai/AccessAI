# Sign clips to record: 24 in all

Each clip replaces the empty file with the **same name** in `frontend/assets/signs/fsl/` or `frontend/assets/signs/asl/`.

**How to record**
- Hold the phone sideways. Show the signer from the waist up, on a plain background.
- Keep each clip 3 seconds or less, with one sign per clip. Start and end with your hands down.
- Use `.mp4`. Pick the lowest video quality your phone offers (720p or less).
- Sign it the way its steps in `frontend/src/learn/content.ts` say. If you sign it differently, tick nothing and tell the owner the right steps.
