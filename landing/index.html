Who it's for

Made for the way you communicate

Tell AccessAI what you need when you sign up, and it sets itself up for you. You can change it any time.

  • Deaf or hard of hearing

    See what people say as text, sign to the camera, and get alerts by vibration and flash.

  • Mute or with speech difficulty

    Type, tap a quick reply, or sign. AccessAI says it out loud for you.

  • Blind or low vision

    Laya, the voice assistant, takes you around the app and reads things out loud. Works with TalkBack.

  • Family and friends

    Chat with the people you support and get their SOS alerts the moment they need you.

Features

Everything you need to be understood

Conversation Mode

Talk face to face on one phone. Tap Them and their words show up as text. Tap Me to type, pick a quick reply, or sign, and the app reads it out loud.

Every conversation is saved, so you can look back later.

Sign language, into words

Sign to the camera in Filipino Sign Language or American Sign Language. AccessAI shows its best guesses, and can turn your signed words into a full sentence you can undo.

Spell names letter by letter, and translate what you signed into English or Filipino with one tap.

Signs are read right on your phone. Your video is never saved or sent anywhere.

Signs it can read

  • Filipino Sign Language
  • American Sign Language
  • Fingerspelling (A–Z)

Pick FSL or ASL in Settings, any time.

Laya, a voice assistant that asks first

Say “Hey Laya” while the app is open and tell it where to go or what to do, in English, Filipino, or Taglish. Laya repeats what it understood and waits for your yes before it does anything.

Nothing you say to Laya is saved.

Emergency SOS

Hold the SOS button, shake your phone, or ask Laya. Friends in your SOS circle get a full-screen alert with where you are, and your location keeps updating for 30 minutes.

  • Text your emergency contacts or call 911 in one tap.
  • Friends can answer “I'm on my way” or “I've seen it”.
  • No internet? Your SOS waits and goes out as soon as you're back online.

Friends and live chat

Add friends with a code or a QR code, chat in real time, and see when your message is seen. Any message can be read out loud.

Learn FSL or ASL

Short lessons of four signs each, with clear steps read out loud, a review, and a camera check that tells you if you got it right.

See who finished the most lessons this week on a friendly board with your friends.

Make it yours

Bigger and bolder text, five color styles including high contrast and color-blind-friendly ones, less motion, and alerts by vibration, flash, or voice.

No signal? No problem

Built to keep working offline

The important parts live on your phone. Messages, SOS alerts and lessons you finish offline wait safely and go out once you're back online, never twice.

  • Typing, quick replies and saved conversations
  • Sign language reading
  • Learn lessons and sign videos
  • Laya's everyday commands
  • SOS by text message and calling 911

What you need

  • Android 7.0 or newer
  • Camera, to read sign language
  • Microphone, for speech to text and Laya
  • Location, to share where you are in an SOS
  • Internet, for chats, friends and SOS alerts to friends. Most other things work offline.
  • To turn speech into text without internet, download your language for your phone's voice typing.

How to install on Android

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. If Google Play Protect asks, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open AccessAI and make your account.
