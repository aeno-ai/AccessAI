About AccessAI

One app for people who are deaf, hard of hearing, mute, blind or have low vision, and for the family and friends who support them.

Why we built it

Talking with others shouldn't depend on whether they know sign language, can hear you, or can see a screen. And when something goes wrong, help should be one tap away, even without a signal.

AccessAI brings Conversation Mode, sign language reading, a voice assistant, sign language lessons and Emergency SOS into one app that keeps working offline.
